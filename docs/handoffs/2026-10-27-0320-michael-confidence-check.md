## What changed
- **One metacognition beat per session, on the card where the answer was.** On the demo arc it lands on the fresh problem after the repair: the student writes the solving line, and before Platy says whether it's right it asks "How sure are you?" Three equal taps (**Guessing**, **Fairly sure**, **Certain**), then "What made you sure?" from a short list ("I put it back in to check", "Every line followed from the one before", "It looked like the last one", "It just felt right", "I'm not sure why", or "I'd rather not say"). A guess sends at once. Then the verdict, Platy's reply to the reason, and on to the next problem. A session that gets to its end without a beat has one before the closing line: "Which step was the key one?" with four step choices when a gap was taught, otherwise how sure they were about the last answer.
- **Two proposed events** (`web/src/protocol.ts`, "Proposed for Connor"): server `confidence_check {problem_id, kind: "how_sure" | "key_step", choices: code[]}` and client `confidence {problem_id, level: 1 | 2 | 3 | null, reason: code | null}`. Codes only, both ways.
- **Words in one table:** `web/src/content/confidence.json` (levels, asks, reason codes → text, step codes → text, the six calibration lines) with `confidence.ts` (`choiceText` is null for an unknown code, `calibrationLine`, `REASON_CODES`, `STEP_CODES`). The student's free text about why goes as an ordinary `child_message`.
- **`ConfidenceCheck.tsx`** (new): a radiogroup of three for how sure, the reasons appearing under it after a tap; a key-step check is one pick with "I'm not sure". `ProblemCard` and `StepSolver` show it in place of the answer buttons or the line box when the check is about their problem, with the box locked and the last line waiting unmarked. Styles in `styles.css` (`.sure*`), motion off under reduced motion.
- **Reducer and hook** (`session/state.ts`, `useTutorSession.ts`): `confidence` (the check on the card, accepted only for the problem on screen, dropped with a new problem, a reset or the session's end) and `calibration` (`{level, right}`: the how_sure level and the verdict that followed, filled from `answer_result` or the solved or marked line, or known already when the beat came after the verdict). `confidence_sent` echoes the chosen words into the chat as the student's and sends the event. `calibration` is carried through `session_end` for Your map and gone at the next start.
- **Your map:** one line from the table when the verdict is known ("You said certain, and you were right. Trust that feeling." / "You said fairly sure, but it wasn't right. Checking it first catches that.").
- **Mock transport** (`transport/mock.ts`): the beat is due on the first right answer after a detour (a returned problem or a fresh `g` problem), never on a probe or the warm-up; the `step_result` or `answer_result` is withheld until the `confidence` event; `closeSession` runs the late beat (key_step when a reteach happened, else how_sure about the last solved problem). During a beat, `answer`, `step` and `hint_requested` are ignored, a chat message gets a fixed reply and doesn't end the beat, `demo("skip")` finishes it (and it counts as had), a pause drops it (and it can fire again after the warm-up). An unknown reason or step code is treated as none. `primer.json` gets the `check_confidence` move and five reasons (`calibrate`, `key_step`, `calibrated`, `underconfident`, `key_step_noted`); `script.ts` gets the asks and replies.
- **R-06:** `confidence.json` is under the fixed-lines test (≤ 2 sentences, ≤ 25 words, no idioms).
- **Acceptance tests:** `mock.test.ts` "confidence check (R-30)" (exactly one beat per session; before the verdict with no verdict or reset in between and nothing said that reveals rightness; the late beat before `session_end`; the key step after a reteach; skip and pause during the beat; chat, answers and lines during it; codes only, canary-free); `state.test.ts` "confidence check (R-30)"; `ConfidenceCheck.test.ts` (three taps, no reasons before a tap, unknown codes skipped, the cards); `map.test.ts` (the calibration line); `script.test.ts` collects the table. ## Decisions and why
- **The beat's moment is the first right answer after a detour,** not the first right answer of the session. Prompt D asked for it to land on the demo arc's fresh problem (the confirm step). "Before confirming a correct answer" with no other condition would spend the one beat on problem 1, and the fresh problem would get none. A student with no detour gets the late beat about the last answer, so every session still has one.
- **`key_step` only as the final beat.** On the demo arc the reteach is followed by the return to the problem, the repair and the fresh problem, where how_sure lands; a key-step question right after the worked example would mean two beats or displace that one. So key_step is the closing beat when a gap was taught and no beat has run (for example the student skipped the problems after the teach). This is a reading of "or after a reteach it asks what the key step was"; easy to move if Connor's policy wants it straight after the teach.
- **`level: null` on a key_step beat** deviates from the prompt's literal `level: 1|2|3`. A key-step pick has no sureness to report, and one event shape for both kinds keeps the client simple. Connor's call.
- **The verdict waits for the answer (R-02).** Nothing said or sent during the beat says whether the line was right: no `step_result`, no `answer_result`, no reset, and the ask doesn't praise. The primer's mastery update does happen before the beat, so the demo panel (laptops, Demo menu) shows the bump while the student is still choosing. That panel is the demo audience's view of what Platy is thinking, not the student's; if you'd rather it waited too, the observe moves after the beat.
- **Skip finishes the beat, pause drops it.** Skip is the demo operator's "move on", so the beat counts as had and the verdict follows. A pause is the student's state changing; the warm-up shouldn't carry a beat, and after it the next right answer gets the beat again. An explicit **End session** runs no beat: the student asked to stop, and a question on the way out would hold them. "Reaches its end" was read as the lesson's end.
- **The calibration line is one line, browser-side,** from the `confidence_sent` level and the verdict that followed, shown only when both are known. Kept small as the prompt asked; nothing about it is stored.
- **Reason codes live in `confidence.json` for now**, five for how sure and four steps. Whether they should be Connor's policy vocabulary is the open question below. The `choices` field is `string[]` on purpose, so his codes can arrive per turn without a schema change (the tool schema stays static per CLAUDE.md; per-turn codes belong in the later system block).
